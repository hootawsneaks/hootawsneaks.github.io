Research

LASA drug name confusability

My undergraduate thesis is on LASA (Look-Alike Sound-Alike) drug name confusability detection for Filipino/Tagalog contexts — building tooling to catch dangerous look-alike, sound-alike medication names before they cause dispensing errors. The pipeline runs a WFST-based grapheme-to-phoneme system, scores candidates with phonetic similarity algorithms (ALINE, BI-SIM), and tunes hyperparameters with a genetic algorithm. It's implemented in Rust with Rayon for the parallel parts.

It's group work under MangoCats, an undergraduate research group at the Center for Language Technologies, DLSU, advised by Nathaniel Oco. Our work was accepted to O-COCOSDA 2025 and presented as a poster.
